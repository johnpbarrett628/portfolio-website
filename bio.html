Professional philosophy and selected work

John Barrett

Marketing Strategy · Business Operations · Marketing Operations · CRM & Analytics

01

Who I Am

I’ve always been drawn to organizations that are building something new or navigating meaningful change. Those environments require curiosity, adaptability, and a willingness to take ownership beyond a single job description. Throughout my career, I’ve found that I do my best work where there’s an opportunity to solve difficult problems, build new capabilities, and leave an organization stronger than I found it.

While my experience spans marketing, business strategy, and operations, I’ve never thought of my work as fitting neatly into one discipline. I enjoy understanding how organizations grow, how people make decisions, and how ideas become practical execution. Whether the challenge is launching a new operation, modernizing a brand, or rebuilding a marketing function, I’m motivated by work that creates lasting value rather than one-time results.

02

How I Think

I approach marketing as both a creative and analytical discipline. Lasting success is built on trust, credibility, and genuinely understanding the people an organization serves. I believe strategy should be informed by evidence, creativity should solve real problems, and good ideas should be measured, refined, and improved over time. Different audiences respond to different messages, and I enjoy using data to better understand those differences rather than relying on assumptions.

I’ve never been interested in creating marketing simply because it’s what everyone else is doing. I enjoy asking why something works, identifying opportunities others may have overlooked, and building solutions that are practical, measurable, and authentic. For me, the goal isn’t simply to launch campaigns. It’s to create systems that continue producing value long after they’re built.

03

How I Work

One thing that has defined my career is a willingness to step into unfamiliar challenges. Many of the skills I’ve developed weren’t learned because they were part of my job description. I learned them because they became necessary to solve the problem in front of me. Whether that meant teaching myself CRM administration, marketing automation, analytics, graphic design, video production, HTML email development, or data analysis, the motivation was always the same: find the best solution for the organization rather than limit myself to what I already knew.

I enjoy working closely with leadership, contributing ideas, and taking ownership of meaningful responsibilities. I work well independently, but I also enjoy collaborating with people who have a clear vision and need someone who can translate that vision into practical execution.

04

What I Want to Build

My goal is to take responsibility for a project I can help shape and own from end to end—something I can pour my full attention, judgment, and energy into making successful. I work hard on every responsibility I accept, but there is another level of commitment that comes from helping carry a vision I believe in and having the room to keep improving it over time.

That goal is also personal. Long before I worked in marketing, I wanted to be a marine biologist. I earned my scuba certification at about twelve years old and later joined Reef Check research trips. Those experiences gave me an early respect for the natural world and a lasting commitment to environmental causes. The work I most want to do combines practical business growth with something genuinely useful—especially when success can also support a healthier, more responsible future.
