Featured case study · Petrosoft

Building a sustainable outbound marketing program.

Focus Marketing operations

Methods Process · Data · Enablement

Environment B2B technology

01 / Challenge

Outbound needed more than a collection of emails.

Different audiences needed different information. Sales needed useful follow-up. Customer and trade show data needed to be organized. The real opportunity was to build a program that could support all of that consistently.

02 / Intervention

I built the process around the work.

That meant researching and enriching contacts, improving segmentation, planning campaigns for a specific purpose, developing the creative, supporting sales follow-up, and reviewing what happened afterward.

03 / Value

The goal was to make the work easier to repeat and improve.

Instead of starting from scratch every time, the team had better audience information, useful campaign types, clearer ways to follow up, and more evidence to guide the next decision.

I enjoy work where there is something meaningful to figure out and build.

That has led me into campaign strategy, design, CRM administration, segmentation, analytics, automation, video, and operations—not because I wanted a longer list of skills, but because those were the tools needed to solve the problem in front of me.
