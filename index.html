01 / Challenge

Good work was moving through a complex system.

Campaigns, content, presentations, audience decisions, and follow-up all depended on inputs from different teams. The opportunity was not simply to produce more—it was to make the path from idea to execution more visible, consistent, and actionable.

02 / Intervention

Design the operating layer, not just the output.

I connected campaign planning, segmentation logic, messaging, asset development, handoffs, and performance review into a coherent working model—so each deliverable carried context forward instead of starting from zero.

03 / Value

Create repeatability without creating rigidity.

The result was a clearer foundation for coordination: reusable frameworks, more intentional audience decisions, stronger continuity across touchpoints, and a feedback loop the team could learn from.
